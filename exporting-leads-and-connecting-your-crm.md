# Exporting leads and connecting your CRM

Export any list as CSV, or push it to Salesforce, HubSpot or Zoho CRM. For sequences, Spona lists work with Salesloft, Lemlist, Outreach and Gong. Field names follow the standard contact schema (first name, last name, title, company, domain, e-mail, mobile, LinkedIn URL, location, ICP score, rationale). A native HubSpot integration and an API key are scheduled for Q4 2026 and Q1 2027.
