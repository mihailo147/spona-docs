# Spona documentation

Spona (SPONA Inc., founded 2023) builds verified B2B sales leads on demand. Instead of filters and spreadsheets, you describe your ideal customer in a chat, by text or voice. Four AI miners take it from there: The Extractor searches 40+ data sources and 400M+ B2B contacts, The Refiner narrows the list to the profile you described, The Verifier checks each lead across its public digital footprint, and The Exporter delivers the result to your CRM or as a file. The first leads arrive in under 4 minutes, with 95% accuracy and under 1% e-mail bounce on Bulk Search. Three search types fit different budgets: Bulk Search (e-mail and LinkedIn, 1 to 9 credits per lead), Standard Search (adds mobile numbers, 3 to 55 credits), and Deep Search (ICP-scored and waterfall-enriched, 60 to 200 credits). Spona works with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesloft, Lemlist, Outreach and Gong, and exports CSV. There are no contracts, no setup fees and no onboarding; a free account includes 3,000 credits, enough for three searches. Customers include Qonto, Kaiko Systems and Zizr. Learn more at https://sponasales.com.
