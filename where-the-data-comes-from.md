# Where the data comes from

Spona combines its own database with 40+ data providers, queried in sequence until a verified e-mail or mobile number is found, and public sources such as company websites and professional profiles. Each lead is checked by The Verifier before export. Spona's accuracy target is 95%; if a delivery falls below it, report it through the help centre and the team investigates. Spona is GDPR- and CCPA-compliant; anyone can request removal of their data at https://sponasales.com/complaint-form?right=delete.
