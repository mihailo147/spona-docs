# Bulk, Standard and Deep Search

Bulk Search returns e-mail and LinkedIn for large lists at 1 to 9 credits per lead, with under 1% e-mail bounce. Standard Search adds verified mobile numbers at 3 to 55 credits. Deep Search scores every lead against your ideal customer profile, verifies it across its public digital footprint and enriches it through a waterfall of 40+ data sources, at 60 to 200 credits per lead, and explains why each lead matches. Use Bulk for volume outreach, Standard for calling, Deep for account-based work.
