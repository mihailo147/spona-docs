# Credits and pricing

Every search starts at 1,000 credits. The price per lead depends on the search type: Bulk Search costs 1 to 9 credits per lead, Standard Search 3 to 55, Deep Search 60 to 200; the exact price is shown before you confirm. Plans: Free (3,000 one-time credits), Pro $99/month (10,500 credits), Premium $499/month (55,000), Expert $999/month (115,000), Enterprise from $20,000/year. Add-on credits cost $390 for 39,000 and expire with the billing cycle. You can cancel or downgrade at any time and keep using your credits until the end of the billing cycle.
